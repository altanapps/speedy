Powell tempers cut expectations ahead of May FOMC

By Sam Fleming and Colby Smith · 4 min read

Investors entered the week pricing in a near-certain rate cut at the Federal Reserve's May 7 meeting, but a measured speech from chair Jay Powell on Monday left rates traders scrambling to recalibrate.

Speaking at the Economic Club of Washington, Powell signaled patience on rate cuts, citing sticky services inflation and a labour market that remains "too tight to be consistent with our 2 per cent objective."

Polymarket contracts on a May cut, which had peaked above 80¢ earlier in the month, fell to 62¢ in the minutes after the speech.
